🌟New Release: Introducing Insights360 for Smarter Communication Analytics

By Kershasp Carnac on July 14, 2025

At Mehery Soccom, we're constantly evolving to help businesses communicate better, smarter, and faster. Our latest release brings powerful analytics and smarter tracking features that take customer engagement to the next level.

We’re excited to unveil two major enhancements for our Lite and Enterprise users:

  • ✅ Insights360 - Business, Agent, and Campaign Analytics Dashboard
  • ✅ Campaign ID API - Reusable Tracking for System-Driven Campaigns

Let’s dive into what’s new and how it can supercharge your business workflows.

📊 Insights360: Powerful Analytics at Your Fingertips

Insights360 is our newly launched dashboard module that provides a 360° analytical view of your organization’s performance across customer engagement, automation, and agent productivity.

🔍 What Can You Track with Insights360?

🔹 Organisational Performance Overview

  • Get a high-level view of your team’s weekly performance
  • Filter data by period, agents, or teams
  • Access metrics like:
    • Agent Summary
    • BOT Summary
    • Lead Messenger performance
    • Avg. response time, conversation duration & response lag
    • Total and unique conversations
    • Graphical breakdown of agent activities

🔹 Agent Analytics

Monitor individual agent performance with precision:

  • Online/offline status tracking
  • Conversations handled (open, resolved, expired)
  • First response time and start lag (including BOT-to-agent handover)
  • Feedback scores from customers

This helps you identify high performers as well as coach poor performers towards enhancing their performance.

🔹 Campaign Statistics

Get channel-wise visibility into campaigns:

  • Total users targeted
  • Messages sent, delivered, read, and replied
  • Failure rates with retry options
  • Template-level analytics (when integrated with API enhancements)

📈 With Insights360, your entire team can stay aligned, informed, and accountable.

🔁 Campaign ID API: Smarter, Reusable Campaign Tracking

We’re also rolling out the Campaign ID API – a developer-friendly enhancement that helps track and group outbound campaigns easily.

💡 Key Benefits:

  • Reusable Campaign IDs: One ID can be reused for a template across a large period of time. This is useful to view analytics for your system driven campaigns.
  • Simplified Tracking: All messages under the same campaign ID are automatically grouped
  • Unified Insights: Integrated directly with Insights360 for detailed analysis

Perfect for businesses running multiple outbound messages daily and looking to track campaign effectiveness across time, templates, and channels.

✅ Why This Release Matters

This update equips your team with deeper insights and better control over communication performance:

  • Boost agent productivity with granular analytics
  • Optimize automation workflows using BOT insights
  • Understand which channels and templates perform best
  • Track campaign outcomes in real-time
  • Make faster, smarter decisions with reliable data

Insights360 and Campaign ID API are available exclusively for Lite and Enterprise subscribers.
