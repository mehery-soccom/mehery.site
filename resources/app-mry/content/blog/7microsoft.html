Microsoft CEO Admits That AI Is Generating Basically No Value

By Kershasp Carnac on February 22, 2025

About time someone said it aloud.

When we speak to CxOs, we often see wide-eyed expectations around AI. The hype is real, but so is the need for a structured, business-driven approach.

Here are some practical guidelines for CEOs on how to effectively embrace AI/ML, Conversational AI, and Cognitive AI. At its core, this is no different from any other operational project an enterprise undertakes:

  • ✅ Identify the problem you want to solve
  • ✅ Assess feasibility—can AI genuinely solve this problem?
  • ✅ Pair business & tech teams—alignment is key
  • ✅ Define known levers impacting the problem
  • ✅ Run a proof of concept (PoC)—start small
  • ✅ Observe new levers that emerge from modeling
  • ✅ Analyze the results and extract insights
  • ✅ Fine-tune & optimize the model iteratively
  • ✅ Measure costs & business impact—AI isn’t free
  • ✅ Scale strategically—land, expand, and refine

If you approach AI with unrealistic expectations or just because of FOMO, the results will be suboptimal. AI comes with costs, and its adoption must be justified by clear business value—not just the buzz around ChatGPT, DeepSeek, or the latest AI model.

Adopt AI for the right reasons, with a strong business case and a focus on tangible outcomes.
